# My Favorite Foreign Series: Squid Game

> "Do you know what people who have no money have in common? They don't know there's any difference between them and garbage." — Oh Il-nam

## Overview
**Squid Game** is a South Korean survival thriller television series created by Hwang Dong-hyuk for Netflix. It centers on a secret contest where 456 players, all deeply in debt, risk their lives playing a series of deadly children's games for a massive cash prize.

## Key Details
* **Main Character:** Seong Gi-hun (Player 456), played by Lee Jung-jae
* **Setting:** South Korea (Secret secluded island facility)
* **Genre:** Survival thriller, Dystopian, Drama, Mystery

### Why It's Captivating
1. High-concept, tense, and unpredictable psychological survival challenges.
2. Deep social commentary on wealth inequality, capitalism, and human desperation.
3. Complex, multi-dimensional characters that make you care deeply about their survival.
